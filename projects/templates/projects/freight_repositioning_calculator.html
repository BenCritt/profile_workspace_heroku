How This Tool Works

Effective rate: a posted linehaul only pays the loaded miles, but the carrier drives the deadhead-in too. Effective RPM spreads the same revenue over every mile of the move, and the roundtrip view extends that math through the reload leg — deadhead-to-reload plus the next load — so the whole trip gets priced, not just the visible one.

Two floors, one zone: each floor is carrier cost plus required profit per mile — the this-load floor over just this move's miles, the roundtrip floor over all miles net of expected reload revenue. The negotiation zone runs between them: a positive gap is the dead-market premium a carrier needs to haul into a weak reload market, a negative gap is the reload-market discount a strong destination lets you capture, and the verdict places an actual ask against that zone.

Related tools: Already know your empty miles? Cost them first with the Deadhead Mileage Calculator — this tool prices what those miles mean for the buy rate. Once a load is booked, run it back through the Freight Margin & Gross Profit Calculator to confirm the margin held. For lane-level rate benchmarking beyond a single load, see the Freight Lane Rate-Per-Mile Analyzer, and pair any of these with the Fuel Surcharge Calculator when a lane's FSC needs to be broken out separately from the all-in buy rate used here.

Estimates are computed entirely from the assumptions you enter above; no live market data is sourced or implied. Carrier operating costs vary widely — the defaults are editable placeholders, not benchmarks. Actual rates depend on market conditions, capacity, and the governing broker-carrier agreement.
