How This Tool Works

Margin is always calculated on revenue, not markup on cost: gross profit divided by the all-in customer total. The Max Buy Ladder shows the highest rate you can pay a carrier at each margin tier, holding fuel surcharge, accessorials, and detention constant - linehaul is the lever you negotiate.

Walk-Away Line: your contingency/risk reserve comes off the top before this number is calculated, so it reflects the true floor after risk provisions - not just breakeven on paper.

Related tools: check your economics on a load that's already booked with the Freight Margin & Gross Profit Calculator. Benchmark your linehaul rate against the lane with the Freight Lane Rate-Per-Mile Analyzer. Model the fuel surcharge on its own with the Fuel Surcharge Calculator, and price detention exposure with the Detention & Layover Fee Calculator.

Rate estimates only. Actual rates depend on market conditions, capacity, and your governing broker-carrier agreement. This tool computes from the inputs you provide and does not source live market rate data.
